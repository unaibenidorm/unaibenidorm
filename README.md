## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🖥️ [GnomeVantage](https://github.com/unaibenidorm/GnomeVantage)

> Control your Lenovo Legion / Ideapad hardware directly from GNOME Shell

A native GNOME extension that brings full hardware control to your desktop —
manage **battery conservation mode**, **fast charging**, **hybrid graphics**, and more
directly from a tray icon or the Quick Settings panel. No Windows tools, no workarounds.

---

### 🌐 [WarpGnome](https://github.com/unaibenidorm/WarpGnome)

> One-click Cloudflare WARP toggle in GNOME Quick Settings

Seamlessly integrates Cloudflare WARP into the GNOME Quick Settings panel.
Connect or disconnect with a single click — no extra apps, no terminal needed.
